# Changelog

## 0.3.0 — 2026-11-11

### Campaigns

- Play story-driven campaigns against the AI, with illustrated introductions and mission briefings.
- Unlock the next mission after a victory, retry after a defeat, and receive a campaign completion message.
- Save and resume campaign progress locally or through portable save files.
- Use the AI difficulty defined by each mission.
- Load campaign files directly or select published campaigns from the campaign catalogue.
- Include the first campaign, The Road to North Pass.

### Author Studio

- Create and edit mission terrain, roads, units, combat attributes, starting funds and AI difficulty.
- Add story text and images, arrange missions, and manage multiple campaigns.
- Import and export mission and campaign files, undo edits, and test missions against the AI.
- Keep author tools in a separate local application and build, outside the public game package.

### Combat and camera

- Add a combat introduction with a camera move, sequential unit enlargement and an approach animation.
- Play a fanfare as combatants approach each other.
- Follow AI troop movements individually and focus on headquarters when the AI purchases units.
- Observe individual opponent moves and animated combat in two-player online games.

### Packaging

- Synchronize the package version and the in-game About dialog at 0.3.0.
- Document campaign publication, image locations and the author workflow.
